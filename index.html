Focus areas

Research

Health Economics

Economic Evaluation Alongside Trials

Cost-effectiveness and cost-utility analyses of clinical interventions in Switzerland and Europe — anticoagulation, SGLT2 inhibitors, GLP-1 receptor agonists, and public health strategies.

Epidemiology

Diabetes & Cardiovascular Disease

Associations between diabetes mellitus, atherosclerosis, myocardial fibrosis, and heart failure using hospital cohort data, general population cohorts, and the UK Biobank.

Evidence Synthesis

Systematic Reviews & Meta-Analysis

Methodological and applied reviews in cardiology, nutrition epidemiology, and migrant health. Co-author of published frameworks for qualitative synthesis.

HTA

Health Technology Assessment

Evidence generation and value communication for pharmaceuticals and devices. Co-founder of CrunchLab Health Analytics, supporting HTA and regulatory submissions in the Philippines.
